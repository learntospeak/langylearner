Use this structure for persuasive, opinion and essay writing.

Simple essay structure:
  1. Introduction: Say your opinion or main idea.
  2. Reason 1: Give your first reason and an example.
  3. Reason 2: Give your second reason and an example.
  4. Conclusion: Restate your main idea in a strong final sentence.
