Mini-quest flow: clear each task to unlock the next and earn a shrine seal.

Goal

Target time: 15-20 minutes total.

Quest steps
Step 1

Vowel intro

Say each vowel once, steady.

Five pure vowels. No sliding.

あA
いI
うU
えE
おO
Step 2

Vowel clarity

Long vowels are longer, not different sounds.

Short vs long changes meaning. Keep vowels pure with no diphthongs.

Example: obaasan (grandmother) vs obasan (aunt).

Tip: hold the vowel for an extra beat instead of changing the sound.
Step 3

Mora beats

Clap once per beat.

One kana = one beat. Long vowels add a beat. Final n adds a beat.

Step 4

Vowel Wash mini-game

Clean 3 hits to unlock the next step.

Tap Listen to hear a vowel.

Goal: 3 clean hits.
